# Comu, I'm

I'm Comu, a RISC-V bare-metal board in your USB port! I have a CH32V203 RV32IMAC with 20 kilobytes of RAM and 248 kilobytes of flash, more than enough for most USB applications. I have four contact pads that can easily be used to make buttons, and two leds that go blink blink!

I'm going to be available shortly for [order now on Crowd Supply](https://www.crowdsupply.com/icy-electronics/comu).
